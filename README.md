ELMS is a simple dashboard app that helps people explore labour market data across Europe.

Live app: https://euro-labor-pulse.lovable.app/

## About the Project

This project shows different labour market statistics in a visual and easy-to-understand way.

Instead of looking at large tables, users can use the dashboard to compare countries, filter by year and sex, and understand trends through charts, KPI cards, and maps.

The goal of the project is to make European labour market data easier to explore and explain.

## What the Dashboard Includes

The app has five main dashboard pages:

- **Employment Rate by Sex**  
  Shows how many people are employed in different European countries.

- **Job Tenure by Sex**  
  Shows how long people usually stay in their current job.

- **Mean Weekly Hours Worked per Employee by Sex**  
  Shows how many hours people usually work each week.

- **Gender Pay Gap**  
  Shows the difference between men’s and women’s average earnings.

- **In-Work At-Risk-of-Poverty Rate by Sex**  
  Shows how many employed people are still at risk of poverty.

## Features

- Interactive filters
- KPI cards
- Country comparison charts
- Trend charts over time
- Europe map visualizations
- Clean and simple dashboard design
- Short explanations for each dataset

## Data

The project uses five cleaned CSV datasets based on labour market data from Eurostat and ILO sources.

The datasets cover:

- employment rate
- job tenure
- weekly working hours
- gender pay gap
- in-work poverty risk

## Why I Made This

I wanted to create a dashboard that makes labour market data easier to understand.

This project helps users see differences between countries, compare women and men, and understand how labour market indicators change over time.
